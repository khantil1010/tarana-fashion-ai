Tarana Fashion AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026
Personal-use application

Tarana Fashion AI is a personal research application currently being developed for individual use. It is not an official Pinterest product and is not affiliated with Pinterest.

1. Information We Access

Tarana Fashion AI may access information from the authenticated Pinterest account that has explicitly authorized the application through Pinterest's official API.

Depending on the permissions granted by Pinterest and the functionality being tested, this may include Pins, Boards, associated metadata, and Pinterest account information required to operate the application.

2. How Pinterest Data Is Used

Pinterest data is used for personal fashion research and analysis. The application may organize and analyze visual fashion references using artificial intelligence.

AI analysis may identify characteristics such as garments, colors, fabrics, patterns, silhouettes, styling, occasions, and seasonal fashion attributes.

The information may also be indexed to allow semantic search, categorization, comparison, and trend research within the application's personal research dataset.

3. Data Storage

Data used by the application may be temporarily or persistently stored for the purpose of providing the application's research functionality.

The application is designed for personal use and does not intentionally make the authenticated user's Pinterest data publicly available through the application.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Tarana Fashion AI may use artificial intelligence services to analyze text and visual content obtained through authorized Pinterest access.

Data sent to an AI service will be limited to information necessary for the requested analysis and functionality.

5. Sharing of Information

Tarana Fashion AI does not sell personal information or Pinterest account data.

Information may be processed by technical service providers, including hosting, database, or artificial intelligence services, when required to operate the application.

6. Pinterest

Tarana Fashion AI uses Pinterest's developer platform and authorized API access where applicable.

Pinterest is an independent third-party service. Tarana Fashion AI is not operated by, sponsored by, or officially affiliated with Pinterest.

7. User Control

The authenticated Pinterest user may revoke the application's access through the applicable Pinterest account settings or authorization controls.

If access is revoked, the application will no longer be able to obtain new data through that authorization.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures will be used to protect application credentials and stored information. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Tarana Fashion AI develops and its functionality changes. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
